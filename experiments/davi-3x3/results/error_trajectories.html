1. Corrected macro-MAE over training step

Macro is recomputed per record from v_star_mae/d{1..6} (mean across present layers). The recorded macro_v_star_mae field is ignored because some evals in the smoke run erroneously included v_star_mae/d0 in the average; the renderer derives from per-layer values to avoid that contamination. y=1.0 is a scale anchor only (not a 3x3 acceptance gate).

2. Per-V*-layer MAE — bounded oracle (d=1..6)

One mini-chart per V*-true depth from the bounded BFS oracle (K=6). Cells are populated only when at least one walk landed on a state with that V*-true depth in the eval batch — sparse at deeper layers early in training. Dashed grey reference at MAE=1.

3. Per walk-depth pred_mean — banded

For each walk-depth d∈{1..14}, the network's mean predicted V* over states reached by random walks of length d. The dashed grey reference at y = depth shows where the prediction would sit if the network identified each walk's length as its V*. In practice walks fold back so true V* is ≤ d; the line should rise over training and sit somewhat below the reference.

4. Per walk-depth pred_std — banded

Spread of predictions per walk-depth. Useful as a coarse calibration signal; very low std at deep walks usually means the network collapsed onto a constant.

5. Beam capability (post-hoc, from beam_eval_focused.json)

Beam(width=256) solve rates at the captured checkpoint(s). Two panels: per V*-true depth (d=1..6, BFS oracle) and per walk-depth (d=1..14). Live solve_rate_dN / avg_solve_len_dN are intentionally NOT captured during 3x3 training — beam cost would slow the training loop, per plan §P1c. The capability picture lives here, post-hoc.
