Block 1B — 2D move animation preview (P0 rev5.2)

Ribbon-with-extension model. Each move animates as three concurrent parts on one timeline: (1) the turned face's 9 stickers rotate ±90° around the face center; (2) the affected edge stickers form a rigid "ribbon" that slides in a single direction (e.g. R's primary ribbon on col 5 slides UP); off-canonical-net source stickers are drawn as visible "extensions" so you can SEE where they come from; (3) for F/F'/B/B' the edge cycle is a closed ring around F-center — those use a rotation primitive (12 stickers rotate 90°) with no extension. For R/R'/L/L' a secondary ribbon at B's near-F column slides in the OPPOSITE direction — the content wrapping over the top/bottom of the cube from U/D arrives at B from above/below B's face. An inverse-opacity overlay dims the region outside the cross silhouette so extensions read as ghosts; rotating face and ring layers render ABOVE the overlay so they never get dimmed mid-rotation. Use the Cross mask checkbox to toggle the dimming.
