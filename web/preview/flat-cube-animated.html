Per-move panels (12 QTM from solved)

Ribbon-with-extension model. Each panel animates one move from solved: (1) the turned face's 9 stickers rotate ±90° around the face center; (2) the affected edge stickers form a rigid "ribbon" that slides; off-net source stickers are drawn as extensions; (3) F/B edge cycles are closed rings around F-center so they use a rotation primitive instead of a slide. R/L moves have a secondary ribbon at B's near-F column that slides OPPOSITE the primary direction (the U/D wrap-around content arrives at B from above/below). Default = cross-silhouette clipped (production view); toggle Show extensions for the debug view with the dimming overlay.
