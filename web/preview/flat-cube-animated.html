Block 1B — 2D move animation preview (P0 eyeball gate)

Each panel shows the pre-move state on load. Click a "replay" button (or "Play all") to run the cycle: 400 ms hold on the pre-move state, 600 ms animation, settle on the post-move state. Conveyor model: on each affected adjacent face, the 3 active-edge stickers + an offscreen 3-strip of incoming new colors translate together along the active edge's axis. Old slides off one end of the edge; new slides on from the other end. Both strips are clipped by the face's 3×3 region, so motion is always visually local to that face. The turned face's 9 stickers rotate as a group around the face center; corners pop outside the face during rotation (intentionally not clipped).
